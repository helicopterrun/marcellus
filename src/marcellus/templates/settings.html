Event alignment

Frigate stamps events from the detect stream and recordings from the record stream — two separate camera connections that can sit a few seconds apart, which makes event bars miss their pictures on the scrub timeline. Measuring compares event thumbnails against the recordings (takes a few minutes) and suggests a per-camera correction; Apply takes effect on the next timeline refresh. Setting detect.annotation_offset in Frigate's own config works too and wins over values applied here.
